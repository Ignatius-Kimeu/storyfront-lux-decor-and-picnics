About Lux

Run by Miriam, on the coast.

Lux Decor & Picnics is a mobile decor and picnic styling service working across Mombasa and Nairobi. Miriam takes the brief, designs the setup, sources the flowers and props, and is on site with the team to build it — which is why the reviews above keep mentioning short notice and things arriving exactly as imagined.

The work runs from a two-person Valentine's teepee on the sand through to full traditional ceremonies and beachfront wedding receptions. Boat and canoe picnics on the Shanzu and Mtwapa creeks have become something of a signature.

What we cover

  • Mombasa and the north coast
  • Nairobi
  • Venue, home, beach, rooftop or on the water
  • Travel further afield quoted on request

How booking works

  • Send the occasion, date, venue and guest count
  • Miriam replies with a design direction and quote
  • Bookings are secured with a deposit
  • Team arrives, sets up, and clears away after

Good to know

  • Short-notice requests are often accommodated
  • Colour palettes agreed before the day
  • Fresh flowers via @petaluxe2026
  • Deposits are non-refundable once a date is held
