Services

Everything we style.

Lux is a mobile service covering Mombasa and Nairobi. Design, hire, flowers, delivery, setup and pack-down are all handled by the team — you only need the venue and the date.

Proposals & engagements

Rose or floral heart arches, neon signage, candle pathways, red-carpet walkways and petal work — on the beach, on a rooftop or indoors.

  • Fresh-rose heart and hoop arches
  • Neon 'Will you marry me?' signage
  • Candle pathways and lanterns
  • Petal carpets and aisle styling

Luxury picnics

Low tables, floor cushions, raffia chargers, coloured glassware, grazing boards and shade. Set up on sand, lawn, rooftop — or inside a decorated canoe on the creek.

  • Beach and garden picnics
  • Boat and canoe picnics (Shanzu, Mtwapa creek)
  • Group tables for 6–20+
  • High tea styling with gilt china

Birthdays & milestones

Balloon garlands, arch and shimmer-wall backdrops, lit marquee numbers, cake tables and full banquet styling for any age.

  • Custom backdrop and garland design
  • Shimmer-sequin and neon walls
  • Lit numbers and letters
  • Themed table styling

Baby showers & gender reveals

Soft neutral or bold themed arches, prop styling, dessert tables and the reveal moment itself.

  • Themed backdrop arches
  • Dessert and gift table styling
  • Prop hire (bears, signage, plinths)
  • Reveal staging

Weddings & traditional ceremonies

Beachfront aisles, drape backdrops, head tables, cultural staging with woven trays and peacock chairs, and full reception layouts.

  • Beach ceremony aisles and drape
  • Head-table and banquet styling
  • Traditional ceremony backdrops
  • Full reception floor layouts

Room decor & surprises

Ceiling balloon installations, petal work, candle styling and personalised keepsakes — the packaged service below.

  • Ceiling balloon installations
  • Rose petal styling
  • Candle and mood lighting
  • Personalised cards and gift boxes
